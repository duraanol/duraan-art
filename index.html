Commissions

What I take on:

Character art
Original characters, D&D and TTRPG players, book and game characters. Bust or full body, inked, with flat color on request.
Tattoo flash
American traditional blackwork. Single designs or a full sheet. You get a clean, print-ready file your artist can work from.
Environments
Places and scenes: a city gate, a shrine, a dungeon room, a landscape from your story or campaign.
Fantasy creatures
Monsters, beasts and spirits, designed from your description or from scratch.
Custom pieces
Anything that doesn't fit the above. These start with a short consult so we agree on scope, size and price before I draw a line.

Send a short description and any references to hello@duraan.art. I reply within a few days with a quote and a timeline.

About

I draw in Orange County, California. Most pieces start on paper with graphite and a calligraphic ink pen, then get finished in Procreate. I build worlds on the side and a lot of the characters here come from them.

Elsewhere: duraanolomi.com, LinkedIn.
