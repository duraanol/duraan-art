How flash works

Pick a design
Every piece on this page is drawn to be tattooed. Tell me which one and roughly where on the body it's going.
Get the file
You get a clean, print-ready version at tattoo scale, sized for the placement, that your artist can stencil from.
Want it changed?
Small tweaks are included. Bigger changes become a custom piece; see commissions.
